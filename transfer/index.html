PING FM1

TransferManual

Load up to eight sounds into the FM-1, record what it plays, and save its pattern as MIDI. Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 on USB.

Sending a bank replaces the sounds stored on the FM-1, so save anything you want to keep first, and leave the FM-1 on USB until the page says it is done. Load only audio you have the right to use. The bank

Nothing yet. The bank holds 73,728 bytes: about 4.6 s at 32 kHz, 9 s at 16 kHz.
A lower rate makes room; 32 kHz for hits, 16 kHz for long material.

Record the FM-1

The FM-1 is a USB audio input. Pick it, press record, play; stop gives you a WAV.

The pattern as MIDI

Saves the four tracks of the pattern now on the FM-1 as a MIDI file, a track each on channels 1 to 4, at its tempo.

Tracks shorter than this repeat. CHANCE steps always play; parameter locks are not in the file.

The project, for the plugin

Saves the project on the FM-1 (its sounds, effects, patterns, song and samples) as a file the PING OS-1 plugin opens in a DAW. Press SAVE on the FM-1 first: the file holds the project as it was last saved.

Nothing here leaves this computer: the page talks to the FM-1 over USB only. Built on fm1-base's transfer code (Charles Vestal, MPL-2.0).
