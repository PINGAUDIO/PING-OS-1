Install and Transfer open once you accept the terms below. Use Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 on USB.
SOUND69 sound models: 49 drums and 20 synths. A supplemental sampler and granular engine. A whole drum kit on one track.
EFFECTS23 effects, each drawn live on the screen. Four on every track, four on the master.
STEPSEight note lanes a track. Parameter locks: any knob, set step by step. Ratchets, chance, ties, micro timing.
GENERATEAn algorithmic line generator that writes bass lines and melodies in key. Autopilot, which keeps varying a playing pattern by itself.
KEYKey lock. One key and scale holds every track in tune.
MOVEFour modulators a track. Four macros, each moving several settings at once.
SONG16 patterns a project, chained in song mode.
LIVEPerformance mode: the master effects on four knobs, with a stutter under your thumb.
SEEA 3D visualizer of the sound. A custom theme colour: the whole screen in any hue you pick.
DAWThe same engine as a VST3 plugin: instrument, effect and MIDI sequencer. A project made on the FM-1 opens in the DAW.
* PING OS-1 is an original operating system for the FM-1 by PING AUDIO, with an original interface, sequencer, drum kit and 49 drum models, 23 effects, modulators, and synth models of its own, among them ANALOG, ACID and RIPPLE. It also includes open-source work by others, used under their licences and with thanks. Ten of the synth models are ports: ORGAN, PHASE, TRIO, LOFI and FM6 come from Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments), with FM6 built on msfa, the core of Dexed; FOLD, VOICE, CHORD, STRING and MODAL come from Plaits by Emilie Gillet (Mutable Instruments). It runs on fm1-base and felucca-bsp, the open groundwork for FM-1 firmware. The typeface is Jost by indestructible type. The full notices and licences are published with the source.
